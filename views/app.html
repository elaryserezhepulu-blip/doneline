Quick-add language

  • Dates: today, tomorrow, friday, next week, weekend, 5 dec, 15.12, in 3 days, in 2 hours, завтра
  • Times: 7pm, 18:30, at 9, noon, tonight
  • Repeat: every day, weekdays, every monday, every other week, monthly
  • Priority: !!! or !1 = high, !! = medium, ! = low
  • Tags: #uni #home   People: @ali
  • Click ✕ on a detected chip to keep that word as plain text.
  • Paste a multi-line list into the box to add many tasks at once.
